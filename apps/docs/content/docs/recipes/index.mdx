---
title: Recipes
description: Larger copyable examples that combine components, motion, drawing, gestures, and interface state.
---

Recipes are complete visual studies rather than single controls. They can use
Reanimated, Gesture Handler, Skia, or another focused dependency when the idea
benefits from it.

## The review app

The [Showcase app](/docs/showcase-app) is the native review surface for the
library. It contains the current components and interactions and will become
the home for larger recipes as they are added.

The first standalone recipe will include its complete source, dependency list,
implementation prompt, and a breakdown of the techniques involved.

## The boundary

- **Components** are small interface foundations.
- **Interactions** isolate a reusable behavior or transition.
- **Recipes** combine multiple techniques into a distinctive copyable example.
