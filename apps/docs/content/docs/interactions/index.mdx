---
title: Interactions
description: Focused pieces of behavior where motion communicates change or responds to input.
---

Interactions sit between foundational components and full recipes. Each one
studies a specific transition, gesture, or piece of feedback in isolation.

New motion patterns will land here as they are ready to be copied and adapted.

## What belongs here

An interaction should solve one recognizable interface problem and be useful
outside the demo where it was created. Larger compositions belong in
[Recipes](/docs/recipes).
