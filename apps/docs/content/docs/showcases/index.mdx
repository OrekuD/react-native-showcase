---
title: Showcases
description: Larger copyable experiments that combine motion, drawing, gestures, and interface state.
---

Showcases are complete visual studies rather than single controls. They can use
Reanimated, Gesture Handler, Skia, or other focused dependencies when the idea
benefits from them.

## The review app

The [Showcase app](/docs/showcase-app) is the native review surface for the
library. It contains the current components and interactions and will become
the home for larger visual experiments as they are added.

The first standalone showcase will be added after the component and interaction
format is settled. Those pages will include the complete source, dependency
list, implementation prompt, and a breakdown of the techniques involved.

## The boundary

- **Components** are small interface foundations.
- **Interactions** isolate a reusable behavior or transition.
- **Showcases** combine multiple techniques into a distinctive experience.
